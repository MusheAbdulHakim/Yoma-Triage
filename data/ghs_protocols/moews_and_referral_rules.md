# Ghana Health Service Emergency Referral & MOEWS Protocol

## 1. MOEWS Thresholds (Modified Obstetric Early Warning Score)
- Systolic BP >= 160 mmHg OR Diastolic BP >= 110 mmHg: SCORE = 3 (Red Flag - Critical Hypertensive Emergency). Initiate IV Magnesium Sulfate loading dose at CHPS level before transfer.
- Heart Rate >= 120 bpm: SCORE = 3 (Red Flag - Severe Infection / Hemorrhage risk).
- Respiratory Rate >= 30 bpm: SCORE = 3 (Red Flag - Respiratory Distress).
- Total Score >= 6: CRITICAL RISK. Immediate emergency 2G SMS dispatch required.

## 2. Northern Region Transport Protocol (Networks of Practice)
- CHPS compounds in rural sub-districts must route emergency transfers via local Motor-King tricycle ambulances when district hospital ambulances are unavailable.
- Automated Mobile Money (MoMo) fuel stipend of 50 GHS (30% advance) is disbursed immediately upon driver USSD acceptance.
- Remaining 70% balance is unlocked when receiving hospital scans the referral QR code upon arrival.
